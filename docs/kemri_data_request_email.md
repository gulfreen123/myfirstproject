**Why the Asembo data specifically.** Our modelling region currently lacks a

Before applying the framework to our own setting, we need to demonstrate that our
implementation reproduces published results on a well-characterised benchmark. The Asembo

observations spanning a documented ITN scale-up (~75% to 93–98%), which is exactly the
signal required to verify that an intervention compartment is correctly implemented.

1. Reproduce the genetic-algorithm calibration of the 22 VECTRI vector parameters

*Anopheles gambiae s.s.* / *Plasmodium falciparum* in the Siaya hydro-ecology and are not

## Before you send

- [ ] Replace every `[BRACKET]` — a request with placeholders reads as a mass mailing.
- [ ] Send from your **institutional** address, not a personal one.
- [ ] Name your actual South Asian target region — vague geography weakens the request.
- [ ] Attach or offer: local ethics approval, a one-page protocol, supervisor's endorsement.
- [ ] Expect a **formal committee application**, not just an email reply. This message
      opens the process; it does not complete it. Allow weeks to months.
- [ ] CC your supervisor / PI if you are a student.

## Do not

- Do not claim affiliation, funding, or approvals you do not have.
- Do not imply the Siaya parameters transfer to South Asia — they do not, and a reviewer
  from KEMRI will know that immediately. Framing it as methodological validation is both
  honest and a stronger case.
